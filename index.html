The race

STRAY is a student-run charity ultra based at IMD Lausanne. Eight runners will attempt an untimed, continuous loop around the lake. Through the day and through the night.

Route · counterclockwiseGPX 166.9 km

From IMD east through Lavaux and the Riviera, across to the French shore at St-Gingolph, west along the French side to Geneva, then home along La Côte.

02 — Why STRAY

Stray from the norm

Mission

“Stray from the norm: promoting community through physical activity to push human limits together.”

01

Together

Movement brings people together. Classmates, running clubs, families, people from the city. One loop, one weekend, one shared effort.

02

By choice

We chose something hard. 165 km without stopping is a choice we made.

03

For them

Many children and families face hard things they never chose. A race is not that, and we don't pretend it is. It is a way to show up for them and raise money for the people who are there every day.

04

For Lausanne

IMD's class lives in Lausanne for a year. This is how we give something back to the city.

The beneficiary · Lausanne
Years beside children in hospital
25+

Fondation Planètes Enfants Malades runs activities for children and teenagers in hospital, and for their families: sport, storytelling, music, a programme for teens, family support and more.

    Who

    MBA and EMBA classes, IMD faculty, staff, academia and individuals.

    Monday 30 November 2026, after the runners finish. You will receive an email with payment details. It all goes to Fondation Planètes Enfants Malades, through the foundation TWINT code with the comment “Charity Ultra”.
